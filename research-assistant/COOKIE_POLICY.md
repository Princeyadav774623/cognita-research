# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how the **Multi-Agent Deep Research Platform** uses cookies and similar storage technologies.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies and local storage tokens are small text files stored on your browser to maintain application state, session authentication, and theme preferences.

---

## 2. Cookies We Use
- **Strictly Necessary / Authentication**:
  - `sb-access-token` / `sb-refresh-token`: Used strictly to authenticate your JWT session securely with Supabase Auth.
- **Security & Rate Limiting**:
  - Encrypted IP signature tokens used by the ASGI rate limiter to protect against brute-force spam and DDoS attacks.

---

## 3. Third-Party Cookies & Analytics
- We do **NOT** use tracking cookies, third-party advertising pixels (e.g. Meta Pixel, Google AdSense), or cross-site data broker trackers.
